Plan where your usual skincare products will be when you need them. A training calendar should help with packing and timing; it should not automatically add extra cleanses or a new set of cosmetics to every workout.

The photograph illustrates planning, not a tested skincare routine.

## Start with the routine you already tolerate

Record when you normally wash, moisturize and use sun protection, then add your training time and journey home. The [American Academy of Dermatology's face-washing guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/face-washing-101) recommends gentle cleansing, avoiding scrubbing, and washing after heavy sweating. It generally advises limiting washing to twice daily and after sweating; a calendar is not a reason to cleanse at every time slot.

Follow your sunscreen's application and reapplication instructions, including those for sweating or swimming. Your trip to and from an indoor gym may still involve time outdoors.

## A compact planning sheet

| Question | Your note |
| --- | --- |
| Where will I train? | Indoors, outdoors or both |
| Where can I wash afterwards? | Home or an available facility |
| What needs to travel with me? | Only the products and clean towel I will use |
| What can stay at home? | Optional or duplicate products |
| What needs cleaning afterwards? | Towel, used clothing, bag or containers as applicable |

## Example: training before going home

For a hypothetical evening session, check the washing facilities and pack only what you expect to use there. Do not schedule another cleanse shortly afterwards solely because the calendar has a separate “evening” row. Adapt the plan to your skin and any professional advice you follow.

Keep containers closed and follow their storage instructions. Avoid leaving products in unsuitable heat, and check the facility's rules about toiletries. Refill and clean the kit according to the relevant product and container instructions.

## Keep optional steps optional

A gym kit does not require a separate toner, serum and eye product. Our [gym-bag reset checklist](/gym-bag-reset-checklist/) covers packing and cleanup. If you are choosing between cleanser types, the [double-cleansing guide](/best-double-cleansing-products/) explains their different roles without making two-step cleansing compulsory.

This is a planning guide. It does not promise to prevent breakouts or treat irritation. Persistent symptoms need individual assessment rather than more steps on a calendar.
