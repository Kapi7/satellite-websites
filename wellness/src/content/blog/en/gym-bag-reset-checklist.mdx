Use this checklist to prepare for your next session. Five minutes is a planning estimate for sorting and packing; washing and drying take extra time.

## Empty and sort

Put the contents on a clean, washable surface. Remove rubbish, separate used clothes and towels for laundry, and return unrelated items to their usual places. Keep essentials such as keys and a wallet in a secure compartment if you need them for your trip.

## Clean according to the care label

Check the bag’s care instructions before wiping or washing it. Leave the bag and reusable containers fully dry before packing them away. A quick wipe is not a substitute for the cleaning process a particular item needs.

## Pack for your next activity

- [ ] Clothing, socks and footwear for the planned session.
- [ ] Clean water bottle, ready to fill.
- [ ] Towel if your activity or facility requires one.
- [ ] Keys, entry card and other travel essentials.
- [ ] Headphones or activity-specific equipment you actually use.
- [ ] Personal toiletries in a closed pouch.
- [ ] A suitable snack if wanted, stored according to its instructions.
- [ ] A separate bag or compartment for used clothing on the way home.

Choose toiletries around your existing routine. You do not need to buy a separate set of skincare products merely to fill a gym bag. ## A simple reset example

After a swim, remove the wet kit and towel, deal with them according to their care instructions, and let the bag dry. Then pack for the next planned activity, which may need different equipment. Keep a short list in the bag if you repeatedly forget one item.

Before leaving, check the things that change: the activity, the weather, the facility’s requirements and your journey home. Use the checklist as a starting point rather than packing everything every time.

*This is an organizational guide. The five-minute estimate is not a timed product test.*
