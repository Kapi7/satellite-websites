## Choose one change, then observe

Choose a single product whose formula and intended role fit what you actually want. If your existing routine already meets that need, there may be no useful addition to make. Avoid buying both just because they appear in a comparison.

The [AAD explains how to test a new skincare product on a small area](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products) before broader use. Follow the product directions and stop using it if you develop a reaction. This guide cannot identify an allergy or replace treatment advice.

## Before you place the order

Check the product name, formula, size, quantity and current checkout total. Record what you already own so you do not accidentally duplicate a treatment. Retailer stock and prices can change; the linked listing is the place to confirm them.
