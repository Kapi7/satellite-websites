## Do you need a second cleansing step?

Do not assume that everyone needs double cleansing, or that every single cleanser is unable to remove sunscreen or makeup. Check the directions and intended use of the product you already own before buying a second one.

The [American Academy of Dermatology's face-washing guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/face-washing-101) recommends a gentle, non-abrasive cleanser, lukewarm water and fingertips rather than scrubbing. It also advises limiting face washing to twice a day and after heavy sweating. These are general cleansing principles, not an endorsement of either product below.

## A shopping worksheet

Before ordering, write down:

- What you use now and the specific problem you want to solve.
- Whether you need one replacement product or two new steps.
- The full product names, sizes and current ingredient lists.
- The total delivered cost and the retailer's returns conditions.

If you cannot explain why the second product belongs in your routine, keep the purchase smaller. Stop using a product that causes irritation and seek individual advice for persistent symptoms.
