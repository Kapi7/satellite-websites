Choose around your first project’s cuts. If it mostly needs curves and shaped cutouts, start by looking at jigsaws. If it mostly needs long straight cuts in boards or sheet material, start with circular saws. Neither tool is an all-purpose winner, and neither substitutes for a safe work setup.

This is a comparison of tool roles and documented specifications, not a hands-on performance test. Prices and included accessories vary by exact model and kit.

## Match the cut before the brand

| Main task | First tool type to investigate | Main buying check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curved shelf edge or shaped cutout | Jigsaw | Blade compatibility, material and cutting capacity |
| Long straight plywood cut | Circular saw | Cutting depth, compatible blade and suitable guide arrangement |
| A mixture of curves and long straight cuts | Plan which cuts dominate, or consider borrowing/renting the second tool | Total setup cost rather than buying two bare tools immediately |
| Occasional short simple cuts | Consider whether a suitable hand tool or a cutting service meets the need | Volume of work and available support space |

[Bosch’s comparison of jigsaws and circular saws](https://www.bosch-professional.com/gb/en/knowledge-innovation/learn-about-bosch-professional-products/jigsaw-vs-circular-saw/) describes the jigsaw’s usefulness for complex cuts and the circular saw’s role in straight cutting. A jigsaw can make straight cuts too, but that does not make it the most convenient choice for every long cut.

## Compare exact models, not just tool categories

Two DeWalt examples illustrate what to read on a listing:

- [DCS334B cordless jigsaw](https://www.dewalt.com/en-us/product/dcs334b/20v-max-xr-cordless-jig-saw-tool-only): the manufacturer lists T-shank blade compatibility and four-position orbital action. That identifies features to compare; it is not a claim that every blade or orbital setting suits every material.
- [DCS565B cordless circular saw](https://www.dewalt.com/en-us/product/dcs565b/20v-max-xr-6-12-brushless-cordless-circular-saw-tool-only): the manufacturer identifies a 6½-inch saw and a 0–50° bevel range. Check cutting capacity at the angle you need rather than treating blade diameter as usable cutting depth.

Both linked versions are labeled tool-only. Confirm battery, charger, blade and other contents on the exact seller offer. A kit with a similar model name may contain different items. These examples are not a ranked recommendation or proof that you should enter a particular battery system.

## Add up the complete setup

Compare the tool, compatible power source, appropriate blades, work support, guide accessories and required protective equipment. If you already own a compatible battery and charger, that can change the comparison. Match the manufacturer's compatibility information rather than relying on voltage branding alone.

Write down your required material, thickness and cut shape before checking a seller listing. For a cordless kit, record the included battery model and charger as well as the saw model. An apparent bargain that cannot do the planned cut is not useful value.

## Read the manual before cutting

Support and secure the workpiece as instructed, keep hands clear of the cutting path and use the protection specified for the job. Disconnect power before blade changes and adjustments as directed. Never defeat a guard or treat a jigsaw as harmless because it has a smaller blade.

Use the model-specific instructions, including the [DCS334 manual](https://www.dewalt.com/GLOBALBOM/QU/DCS334P1/1/Instruction_Manual/EN/N577652_DCS334.pdf) and [DCS565 manual](https://assets.dewalt.com/GLOBALBOM/QU/DCS565B/1/Instruction_Manual/EN/N862094_DCS565.pdf), for permitted materials, blade selection and operating procedures. This buying guide does not teach plunge cutting or replace those instructions.

## A useful first-purchase decision

For a decorative project dominated by curves, make a jigsaw shortlist. For repeated straight cuts in sheet material, make a circular-saw shortlist. If the project does not justify a purchase yet, borrowing, renting or using a cutting service can help you learn what you need before committing to a battery platform.
