Start with the tools you need to store and the route you carry them along. A modular box, small-parts organizer and rolling cabinet solve different problems. This is a buying guide, not a load test or a ranking based on hands-on use.

## Choose the storage type first

| What needs organizing? | Type to compare | Check before buying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screws, small fittings and connectors | Compartment organizer | Small parts stay separated with the lid closed; removable cups fit your parts |
| A drill, charger and hand tools | Deep portable box | Interior dimensions with the tray installed, not just outer dimensions |
| Tools carried on foot | Tool backpack | Loaded weight, pocket layout and whether sharp edges are protected |
| A growing garage collection | Drawer cabinet | Drawer dimensions, load ratings and safe placement on your floor |
| Equipment moved between sites | Modular stack or rolling box | Exact platform compatibility and route through doors or stairs |

## A concrete example: DeWalt DWST17806

The hero photograph shows the **TSTAK VI deep box, DWST17806**. The [DeWalt listing](https://www.dewalt.com/en-us/product/dwst17806/tstak-vi-deep-box) describes a removable tray and side latches that connect TSTAK modules. The box is the product; tools shown elsewhere in a seller's gallery may be illustration only.

DeWalt's [interior-dimension reference](https://support.dewalt.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012660578-What-are-the-interior-dimensions-of-DEWALT-Tstak-drawers) lists the DWST17806 interior as 360 × 270 × 240 mm. Check the tray and your tool's protruding handle or battery before relying on a simple rectangle measurement. Nominal dimensions do not guarantee that every tool of a similar size fits.

TSTAK and other modular storage families are not automatically interchangeable. Verify the exact connection method and any required adapter. Do not assume a waterproof rating from the appearance of the lid or from another product in the same range.

## Make a fit list before ordering

Lay out the items you actually carry. Record the largest item's length, width and height with any fitted battery, plus the charger and accessories. Keep sharp tools protected and follow the storage instructions for tools and batteries.

Compare the total loaded weight with your ability to carry it and the manufacturer's rating. A large box may fit everything but become awkward on stairs. Two smaller boxes can be more practical than one heavy stack.

## Check the exact offer

Confirm the model, included tray or dividers, wheels if pictured, dimensions and return terms. Use the current delivered price; old advertised prices are not a reliable budget. Start with one module if fit is uncertain instead of buying a full system based on a photograph.
