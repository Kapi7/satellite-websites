## Check these five things first

| Check | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Surface and task | The tool and abrasive are intended for your material and job |
| Pad | Diameter, attachment method and extraction-hole pattern |
| Power | Corded or cordless, local supply and the exact package |
| Dust setup | Compatible extraction connection and suitable collection equipment |
| Complete cost | Tool, abrasives, extraction connection and anything not included |

A round pad cannot reach every internal corner. Nor does a random-orbit label guarantee a scratch-free finish. If a small hand-sanding job is enough, you may not need a powered tool at all.

## A concrete kit: DeWalt DWE6423K

DeWalt's [DWE6423K product page](https://www.dewalt.com/en-us/product/dwe6423k/dewalt-5-127-mm-corded-electric-variable-speed-random-orbital-sander-kit) identifies a **corded, 5-inch variable-speed kit**, with a 3 amp motor and an 8,000–12,000 OPM range. OPM means orbits per minute; the speed range is not a measured finish-quality score.

The listed package contains the sander, dust bag and carrying case. The manufacturer photograph above shows that kit. It does not include a vacuum, a battery or every abrasive you will need. Check the seller's current model and contents against the manufacturer reference.

If you are comparing a single-speed model, DeWalt lists the [DWE6421K](https://www.dewalt.com/en-us/product/dwe6421k/5-single-speed-random-orbit-sander-kit-hl-pad) at 12,000 OPM. Decide whether adjustable speed is useful for your planned work rather than assuming that a bigger maximum number means a better purchase.

## Match the discs, not just the diameter

![Manufacturer close-up of a sanding disc being aligned with the pad on the DeWalt DWE6423K](/images/verified/dwe6423k-disc.webp)

*Photo: DeWalt's DWE6423K gallery. This illustrates the disc connection, not a Build Coded sanding test.*

The [DeWalt instruction manual](https://assets.dewalt.com/GLOBALBOM/QU/DWE6423K/1/Instruction_Manual/EN/N412818_DWE6423.pdf) specifies 5-inch hook-and-loop discs with an eight-hole extraction pattern for this model. Align the holes and check the attachment type before ordering a box of discs. A similar diameter alone is insufficient.

Disconnect the tool from power before fitting or removing accessories, as the manual directs. Select abrasives for the material and follow the tool, abrasive and finish instructions. This shopping guide is not a universal grit schedule for every coating or timber.

## Price the extraction connection too

A dust bag and a vacuum connection are different setups. DeWalt's [adapter reference](https://support.dewalt.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012581557-If-I-have-a-vac-with-a-1-hose-that-is-not-made-by-DEWALT-is-there-an-adaptor-that-will-connect-this-sander-to-my-vac) identifies DWV9000 for specified 1¼-inch hoses on these sanders. Check your exact hose, connection and collection equipment rather than assuming any vacuum will plug straight in.

We have not measured how much dust the supplied bag captures. Do not interpret a photograph of a bag as proof that exposure is controlled. Follow the manual and the requirements for the material you are working on. The manual states that these sanders are not for drywall applications; do not choose this kit as a drywall sander.
