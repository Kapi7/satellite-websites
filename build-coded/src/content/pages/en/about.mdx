Build Coded publishes guides to tools, materials, and home improvement projects.

## What we promise

- Our guides combine source research and AI-assisted drafting.
- A product comparison is not evidence of hands-on testing. Look for documented methods and results when a test is claimed.
- Some retailer links are affiliate links. See our [disclosure](/disclosure/) for details.
- Check current specifications, package contents, suitability and prices with the manufacturer or retailer before buying.

## How we research

Build Coded guides are **researched, not lab-tested**. For each product guide or comparison, we:

1. **Start from manufacturer documentation:** product pages, manuals and spec sheets for the exact model and package (tool-only vs kit, board revision, included accessories).
2. **Read owner reviews and published sources** to find common strengths, problems and compatibility questions.
3. **Draft with AI assistance, then edit.** An editor checks the facts against the linked sources, removes claims we can't support, and adds safety notes and links to manuals.
4. **Say what we haven't done.** Unless an article documents a method and results, we have not tested the product hands-on, measured runtime or run strength tests.
5. **Disclose affiliate links.** Some retailer links earn us a commission (see our [disclosure](/disclosure/)). It doesn't change what we recommend, and we don't publish sponsored content.

Always check current specifications, package contents, suitability and price with the manufacturer or retailer before you buy, and follow the manual for your exact model.
