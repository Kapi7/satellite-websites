## Sources and methods

Our guides draw on product listings, ingredient labels and published sources. AI assists parts of drafting and translation. Research-based comparisons are not hands-on tests. A testing claim needs a documented method and supporting evidence.

## Affiliate disclosure

We may earn a commission on purchases through qualified Mirai Skin links. Confirm the current formula, size, shade, availability and total price before ordering. A recommendation does not establish that a product is suitable for everyone.
