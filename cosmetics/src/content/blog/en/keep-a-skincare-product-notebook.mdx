A useful skincare notebook answers a shopping question: **what exactly did I buy, and would I choose that same product again?** A label photo and a few short notes are usually enough. You do not need to copy every ingredient by hand.

## Record the exact product

Save the brand, full product name, size, formula or shade, purchase date and retailer link. Photograph the ingredient label and any batch or expiry information. Two products with similar names can have different formulas or packaging sizes.

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Exact product | Brand, full name and formula or shade |
| Package | Size and number of items in the order |
| Purchase | Retailer, link, date and actual amount paid |
| Use | Opening date and the routine in which you used it |
| Your observations | Texture, scent, convenience and anything you want to discuss with a clinician |
| Next order | Rebuy, compare an alternative, or skip—with your reason |

Keep receipts and label photos together. Record your actual experience in your own words; a notebook can document an observation without proving what caused it.

## Use the notes before reordering

Open the current product listing and check its full name, size and variant against your record. Check the current ingredient label if the formula matters to your choice. Confirm availability and the final checkout total rather than assuming an old price still applies.

For example, Mirai lists [Anua Heartleaf Pore Control Cleansing Oil 200 ml](https://mirai-skin.com/products/anua-heartleaf-pore-control-cleansing-oil-200ml) and [Anua 8 Hyaluronic Acid Hydrating Gentle Foaming Cleanser 150 ml](https://mirai-skin.com/products/anua-8-hyaluronic-acid-hydrating-gentle-foaming-cleanser-150ml). These are different products and sizes. Record the exact one you intend to buy; a shared brand name is insufficient.

If the package size has changed, use our [skincare unit-price worksheet](/compare-skincare-unit-prices/) before deciding whether a larger package offers value. A lower unit price only helps if you want and will use that amount.

## Keep the entry short

A hypothetical entry could read: “Cleanser A, 150 ml, ordered from the saved retailer link; opened 10 September; pump is convenient; check the current size and price before reordering.” Add your real purchase amount and observations when making your own entry.

Review the notebook when something changes or before shopping. It is a purchase and usage record, not a diagnostic tool or evidence that a product caused a skin change.
