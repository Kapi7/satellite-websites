## Compare cost only after deciding on formula

Both packs use millilitres, so unit cost is product price divided by the stated volume. For a **hypothetical calculation**, a 100 ml bottle costing 20 currency units is 0.20 per ml; a 50 ml bottle costing 15 is 0.30 per ml. Those are invented arithmetic examples, not current prices at Mirai.

A lower cost per ml does not establish better results or a suitable formula. Compare the amount you will realistically use and the final delivered total. Avoid buying a second product just to reach a shipping threshold unless the whole basket makes sense for you.

## Choose with realistic expectations

This guide cannot rank absorption time, irritation risk or visible results for your skin. Ingredient lists alone do not establish that one product heals skin, treats scars or reaches deeper layers than the other. If you are seeking treatment for a skin condition, a cosmetic comparison cannot make that choice for you.

For a new product, the [American Academy of Dermatology explains testing skincare products on a small area](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products/). Follow the product directions and seek advice for a persistent reaction. If your current routine works, there is no requirement to add both products.
