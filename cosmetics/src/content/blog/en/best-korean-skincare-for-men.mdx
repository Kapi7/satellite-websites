Choose skincare around your skin's dryness, oiliness and sensitivity rather than assuming that every man needs the same products. A simple routine is easier to evaluate than ten new products at once. This is a listing-based buying guide; we have not tested these products on a panel of men.

## Start with the basics

The [American Academy of Dermatology's guidance for men](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/skin-care-for-men) recommends choosing products for your skin type, gentle cleansing, moisturizing and sun protection. A moisturizer labeled “all in one” does not automatically replace sunscreen. For shaving irritation, use suitable shaving technique and seek advice for persistent symptoms rather than adding more cosmetics.

## Before checkout

Confirm that the product matches the step you need, then read the full ingredient list and directions. Check shipping availability, delivered total and whether you selected a one-time purchase or a subscription. Stock can change after this review.

Introduce one new product at a time so that you can identify a problem if irritation occurs. Neither listing proves that a product will treat acne, prevent hair loss or improve skin firmness. A hair serum is not a necessary face-care step.
