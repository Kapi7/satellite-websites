Dry lips do not need ten new products. Start with the format you will use consistently: a portable balm, a tube treatment or a richer mask. This guide compares three Korean options sold by Mirai Skin and explains when a simple alternative is more sensible.

## 1. TOCOBO Vitamin Nourishing Lip Balm: the portable option

A stick is convenient if a jar or tube makes you less likely to reapply. Mirai lists the [Vitamin Nourishing Lip Balm in 3.5 g](https://mirai-skin.com/products/tocobo-vitamin-nourishing-lip-balm-3-5g). Compare the complete ingredient list with any ingredients you already know irritate your lips. Do not assume that a vitamin claim means the product includes sun protection.

Choose this format for convenience. Skip it if you need a fragrance-free product and cannot verify that the exact formula meets that requirement.

## If your lips are already cracked or irritated

The [American Academy of Dermatology's chapped-lip guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/dry/heal-dry-chapped-lips) recommends non-irritating lip care and identifies white petroleum jelly as a useful option for very dry lips. Cooling, tingling or burning is not proof that a product is working. Avoid picking and scrubbing chapped lips; fragrance and ingredients such as menthol can irritate them.

Use a lip product with SPF 30 or higher outdoors and reapply as directed. The three listings above should not be treated as lip sunscreens without an explicit SPF label. If chapping does not improve after two to three weeks of appropriate care, seek advice from a dermatologist.
