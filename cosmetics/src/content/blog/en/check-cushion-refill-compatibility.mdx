A refill is useful only when it fits your existing case. Similar color, brand or shade names do not confirm that fit. This checklist is based on product identification, not a hands-on compatibility test.

**Affiliate disclosure:** We may earn a commission through Mirai links.

## Identify your current compact

Record the exact brand, product line, size and shade from the case and packaging. A mini, full-size compact and refill are different shopping choices. Photograph the label for your own reference.

## Check the replacement listing

The photograph shows a TIRTIR Red Cushion compact from the linked retailer listing. It is not a photograph of a refill or proof that a replacement fits. The [Mirai Red Cushion 18 g listing](https://mirai-skin.com/products/tirtir-mask-fit-red-cushion-spf40-pa-18g-45shades) identifies the product discussed; we have not verified a compatible refill for it.

Before ordering a refill, find an explicit manufacturer compatibility statement for your exact case and the replacement. Matching shade numbers or a similar shape are insufficient. If that information is missing, ask the seller before paying.

## Compare the complete purchase

Confirm what the package includes, the exact shade, current stock, delivered total and return terms. A refill is not automatically better value. If the case is damaged or compatibility is uncertain, delay the order rather than guessing.

If you are choosing a new cushion instead, use the [TIRTIR shade guide](/tirtir-cushion-foundation-shade-guide/) to separate formula, shade and size decisions. No shade is selected for you.
