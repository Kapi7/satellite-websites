## Start with the exact cushion formula

TIRTIR sells several cushion lines. Red, All Cover, Aura and AI Filter are separate products; a photo of a red compact should not stand in for the entire range. Start by recording the full name of the product you intend to buy.

This guide links specifically to the [TIRTIR Mask Fit Red Cushion, 18 g, at Mirai Skin](https://mirai-skin.com/products/tirtir-mask-fit-red-cushion-spf40-pa-18g-45shades). It does not cover every formula or promise that a shade will look identical across them.

## Make a shade shortlist before opening the basket

Use [TIRTIR's official shade finder](https://tirtir.global/pages/find-your-shade) as one starting point. It offers a Red Cushion shade tool. Treat an online result as a shortlist, rather than a guaranteed match.

Write down the full brand, formula and shade of a foundation that already suits you. Then note what you want to change: depth, undertone, finish or coverage. These are different shopping questions. Wanting less shine does not automatically mean you need a lighter shade.

If you can try the exact formula before buying, compare it in ordinary daylight as well as indoor light. If you cannot, look for clearly identified swatches of the same formula and shade. A filtered photograph or an unnamed compact is a weak basis for a purchase.

## Depth, undertone and finish are separate decisions

Depth describes how light or deep the shade appears. Undertone describes its color direction. Start with both, then compare the finish and coverage you want. A number or letter from another brand is not a universal shade match.

If a swatch changes appearance after application, compare it after it has settled and in more than one light. We have not measured oxidation for this formula, so we do not recommend that everyone automatically buy one shade lighter. A shade finder, swatches and a familiar foundation reference should narrow your options; none guarantees a match.

The cushion's SPF label also does not make a light makeup application equivalent to applying sunscreen as directed.

## When to wait before buying

Pause if you have two plausible shades and no useful reference, or if the only available option is one you already suspect is wrong. Check the retailer's current returns conditions before opening or testing a delivered cosmetic. Do not assume that an opened shade mismatch can be returned.

For example, a shopper who knows their current foundation is too warm should first resolve the undertone question. Buying the next darker number simply because it is available does not answer that question.
