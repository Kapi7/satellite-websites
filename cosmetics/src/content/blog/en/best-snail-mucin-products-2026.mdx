Choose the product's role before choosing a percentage. **COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence, 100 ml**, is an essence. **COSRX Advanced Snail 92 All in One Cream, 100 g tube**, is a moisturizer. They are two different formulas; a higher number in the name does not make one the better product for every routine.

## Know what this comparison can establish

These are cosmetic choices. This page does not establish that either product treats acne, heals wounds, removes scars or prevents a reaction. The [American Academy of Dermatology's product-testing guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products/) explains why even a product selected for your skin type can cause irritation and how to test a new product on a small area.

If you avoid animal-derived ingredients, snail secretion filtrate is a reason to choose a different formula. If you have a relevant allergy or an ongoing skin problem, seek appropriate advice rather than treating a cosmetic label or this guide as an allergy assessment.

Buy the product that fills a role you actually want, in the exact format you intended. You do not need both bottles to have a complete routine.
