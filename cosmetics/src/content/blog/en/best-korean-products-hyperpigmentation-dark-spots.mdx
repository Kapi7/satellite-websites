Shopping for a dark-spot product starts with two questions: do you know what the mark is, and what would this purchase add to the routine you already use? A serum's name or percentage cannot answer either question by itself.

| Am I replacing a serum or adding a cream? | List what you already use; identify the missing role |
| Does a larger percentage mean a better choice? | Check the whole formula and directions; do not rank by the number alone |

The [AAD's guide for darker skin tones](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/routine/fade-dark-spots) emphasizes identifying the cause and avoiding irritation. It recommends tinted, iron-oxide sunscreen with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher and water resistance. This guidance does not endorse the products below.
