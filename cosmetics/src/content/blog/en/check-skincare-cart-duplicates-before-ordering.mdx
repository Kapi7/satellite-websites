Before ordering, compare the exact cart entries with the products already at home. Similar names can describe different sizes or formulas, while a bundle can duplicate a standalone item. This checklist is an organizational aid, not a claim that every pair of similar products is interchangeable.

The credited photograph is contextual stock photography. The products shown are not Mirai recommendations.

## Record what you intend to replace

Write down the brand, full product name, formula or shade, pack size and quantity you need. Use the label on your current product rather than a shortened name from memory. Our [product notebook guide](/keep-a-skincare-product-notebook/) offers a reusable record.

## Compare each cart line

| Check | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Brand and full product name, including any formula suffix |
| Variant | Shade or other selected option |
| Pack | Size, number of units and whether it is a refill or complete product |
| Bundle | Every included item and size, including anything also ordered separately |
| Quantity | Units already owned plus units in the cart |
| Purchase type | One-time order or recurring subscription, as shown by the seller |

Two items can perform a similar role without being duplicates. Decide whether you actually need both; do not infer identical formulas from similar packaging.

## Worked example: a hypothetical bundle

Suppose a fictional bundle lists one 100 ml cleanser and one 50 ml moisturizer, while your cart also contains the same 100 ml cleanser separately. You would receive two cleansers if those descriptions are correct. If you intended to buy one, remove the unwanted cart line and check the updated total. This is an invented arithmetic example, not a real Mirai offer.

## Check the final order summary

Recheck the selected variant, quantities and purchase type after editing the cart. Compare the delivered total rather than assuming that a bundle or larger pack is cheaper. The [unit-price guide](/compare-skincare-unit-prices/) explains how to compare equivalent quantities; it does not establish whether different formulas are substitutes.

If a listing is unclear about its contents, resolve that uncertainty before paying. Keep the order confirmation so you can compare what arrives with what you selected. This guide does not promise a seller's return terms or availability.
