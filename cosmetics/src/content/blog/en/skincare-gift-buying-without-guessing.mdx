A useful skincare gift starts with a product the recipient actually wants. Ask for the full name or a link before buying. If you only know that they “like Korean skincare,” that is not enough to choose their next serum, foundation shade or treatment.

**Quick choice:** Replacing a favourite? Match its exact formula and size. Buying something new? Agree on the product first. Cannot ask without spoiling the surprise? Choose a different kind of gift rather than guessing about their skin.

## Five questions that prevent an unwanted gift

Send this short message: “I'd like to buy you something you'll use. Could you send the product link, the size you prefer, any variant or shade, and when you'd need it?” Then confirm whether it is a replacement or a first try.

| Check | A useful answer |
| --- | --- |
| Full product name | The formula, not just the brand |
| Pack size | For example, 3.5 g stick or 11 ml tube |
| Variant | Exact shade, scent or flavour where applicable |
| Existing experience | Already uses it, or specifically wants to try it |
| Delivery deadline | The date it must arrive, not the day you will order |

A photograph of the recipient's current package can help, but compare its wording with the current listing. Packaging and formulas may change. A similar colour or a familiar brand name is not a reliable match.

## When a surprise is the wrong approach

A “for all skin types” label cannot tell you whether a particular recipient will tolerate a formula. The [American Academy of Dermatology explains how to test new skincare products](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/prevent-skin-problems/test-skin-care-products/), including why a product can still cause irritation despite apparently matching a person's skin type. Let the recipient decide whether and how to introduce it.

If they avoid particular ingredients, are following a treatment plan, or have asked not to receive skincare, respect that preference. Do not buy a treatment product to hint that they should change their skin. A gift card is only an option if the retailer actually offers one for the recipient's country; this guide has not verified a Mirai gift-card service.

## Check the order before paying

Keep the purchase simple: one agreed product, the intended quantity, and the recipient's correct delivery destination. Compare the final delivered total with your budget. Do not increase the basket just to chase a free-shipping threshold without checking the extra cost.

Check whether the basket is a one-time order or a recurring purchase. Read the retailer's current return conditions, including any restrictions once cosmetics are opened, and confirm whether a gift receipt is available. Do not promise the recipient that an opened product can be returned unless the current terms say so.

For a deadline, confirm dispatch and transit information for the actual destination. Stock availability alone does not establish an arrival date. If delivery is uncertain, arrange the gift later rather than relying on an unverified estimate.

## A useful note to include

Write the exact product name and size on the gift note, keep the order details privately, and leave the packaging sealed. A thoughtful message is enough; there is no need to attach promises about anti-ageing, healing or guaranteed results. The successful gift is the product they wanted, delivered as expected.
