Before throwing away an empty bottle, save its full name, size and product link. “The blue Torriden one” is not enough information for a reliable reorder: the same range includes different formulas and product types.

**Quick check:** Match the formula, size, variant and quantity first. Then check today's availability and delivered total. If any of those details differ, pause before paying.

## Save this record while you still have the package

Copy these lines into a private note. Photograph the front label and ingredient panel for your own reference; no account, app or upload is needed.

```text
Brand and full product name:
Product type / formula:
Size per item:
Number of items:
Shade, scent or other variant:
Exact retailer product link:
Photo of my current label saved:
Date I opened this package:
Expiry or period-after-opening information on this package:
Last purchase date and delivered total:
What I want to repeat or change:
```

Keep the opening date separate from the purchase date. Read the actual package instructions instead of assuming that every product lasts the same number of months. Your previous receipt is a useful comparison, not a promise of today's price or formula.

## Example one: the DIVE-IN Serum bottle

The [Torriden brand serum page](https://torriden.us/products/dive-in-serum) identifies **DIVE IN Serum, 50 ml**. Its [DIVE IN Skin Booster](https://torriden.us/products/dive-in-skin-booster) is a different product, listed as **200 ml**. A larger blue bottle is not automatically a better-value replacement for the serum you already use.

For the serum example, your record would say “Torriden DIVE-IN Serum; serum; 50 ml; one bottle.” If the listing instead says Skin Booster, cleansing foam, two bottles or a different size, you have found a different offer. Compare the current full name and ingredients with your record; packaging colour alone cannot establish a formula match.

## Use a four-part check before checkout

| Check | Compare with your record |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Full formula name and any variant |
| Pack | Amount per item multiplied by item count |
| Order | Intended quantity and one-time or recurring purchase |
| Delivery | Destination, delivery information and final total |

A 50 ml single bottle and a two-bottle pack contain different total amounts. Compare unit cost only after establishing that the formula and units match. Include shipping and any charges shown at checkout rather than comparing the product price alone.

## When to stop the reorder

Pause if the product caused an unwanted reaction, you already have enough unopened stock, or the current ingredient list differs in a way you cannot resolve. A past purchase does not prove that a new formula will suit you. Ask the retailer or manufacturer about an unclear change instead of treating the old photograph as proof.

If you are buying for somebody else, ask for their current link and variant. Our [skincare gift checklist](/skincare-gift-buying-without-guessing/) explains what to confirm. A successful reorder is the item you meant to buy, in the amount you need, with terms you understand.
