## Checklist frame: decide before you buy

Walk four gates before the cart:

1. **Goal** — Are you curious about comfort, a “shedding feel,” or how a part line looks under harsh light? Those are cosmetic optics and feel goals. They are not a medical diagnosis of alopecia.
2. **Format** — Rollerball/gel applicators versus droppers change how easy nightly use feels. Medicube’s listing describes a gel serum with a triple-roller style applicator — stick to the current label and Mag’s description; this checklist does not invent applicator science.
3. **Time commitment** — Leave-on scalp steps usually ask for consistent evenings (or Mag’s stated cadence). Judge over **weeks**, not the next morning’s mirror.
4. **Evidence honesty** — Brand claims plus Mag’s editorial log are useful context. They are not a clinical cure. Use Mag for the log; use this page to decide if the category fits you.

If any gate fails — wrong goal, no patience for weeks, or medical red flags — pause the purchase.

Write your answers down if that helps. A one-line note such as “optics under gym lights, can do five nights a week, already read Mag” is enough. If you cannot finish the sentence without adding “I need this to reverse diagnosed loss,” stop and talk to a clinician before shopping.

Also decide what “worth it” means for you: comfort after a week, easier part styling after a month, or simply “I finished the bottle and want another.” Mag’s log can inform expectations; it cannot promise your outcome.
